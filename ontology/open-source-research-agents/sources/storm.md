# STORM and Co-STORM source card

Observed on 2026-08-03 at default-branch commit fb951af7744dab086e34962e9bc6fe878e145f83.

STORM performs Internet research, creates a hierarchical outline, and generates a Wikipedia-like article with citations.

STORM uses perspective-guided questions and simulated conversations to broaden and deepen its research.

Co-STORM supports human participation and maintains a dynamically updated hierarchical mind map as a shared conceptual space.

The documented retrieval modules include web-search providers and VectorRM for user-provided documents.

The repository identifies its software license as MIT.

The mind map is close to a reusable hierarchy, but this source card did not verify a versioned claim-and-evidence ontology that persists across research runs.
